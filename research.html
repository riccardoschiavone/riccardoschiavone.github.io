Riccardo Schiavone, Ph.D.

Research Staff in Telecommunications at the Satellite Networks Department of the Institute of Communications and Navigations of the German Aerospace Center (DLR)

Currently my main focus is on the following topics:

  • Design and decode of low-rate codes for massive machine type communications (mMTC).
  • Short convolutional codes for ultra-reliable low-latency communications (URLLC).
  • Design of communication systems capable of supporting different error targets for the coexistence of several applications in one system. Main technology, channel codes for the unequal message protection problem.
  • Design of protocols for the unsourced multiple access problem.
  • Codes for improving the resilience of wireless networks to power failures using smart grids.
  • High-speed fully software erasure code encoders and decoders for inter-satellite links.
  • Channel coding techniques for the implemention of neural networks in noisy hardware or environments (e.g., radiations in space).
  • Channel codes for distributed computing.
